TechRestock is the B2B wholesale ordering platform built for retail store owners and repair technicians. Restock your store at wholesale rates — order mobile accessories, chargers, cables, screen protection, audio gear, and repair parts in bulk, track what you owe each supplier, and never run out of top sellers.

Instead of travelling to crowded electronics markets, browse wholesale inventory by category, order in master cartons or bulk inner boxes, and submit your order request in seconds.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Shop by category: Browse Chargers & Power, Cables & Adapters, Cases & Covers, Screen Glass, Audio & Earbuds, Power Banks, Car Accessories, Memory & Storage, Repair Parts, and Smart Gadgets — with verified wholesale suppliers in every category.

• Order in bulk: Every item shows minimum order quantities (MOQ) and tiered bulk volume discounts — the larger your batch order, the lower your per-unit cost.

• Order sheet: Build your inventory sheet, review quantities, and send a bulk order request instantly. A TechRestock distributor representative confirms final pricing and delivery options directly with you.

• Supplier ledger: Keep a running credit ledger with each distributor — record goods received on credit and payments made, with outstanding balances updated automatically. Your ledger stays 100% private on your device.

• Stock reminders: Set automated reorder reminders on high-turnover items (fast chargers, Type-C cables, tempered glass) so your store shelves stay fully stocked.

• Offers & Deals: Access bulk clearance deals and distributor promotions in one place.

• Order history: Track past inventory requests and supplier orders effortlessly.

• Account management: Register with your retail store name, update profile settings, and manage account deletion anytime within the app.

PLEASE NOTE

TechRestock is a wholesale ordering and B2B trade inquiry app. Order requests are non-binding inquiries — final price, shipping, and delivery terms are confirmed directly with the TechRestock team.

PRIVACY

We use Firebase Authentication to secure your sign-in and Cloud Firestore for your store profile and order inquiries. Your supplier ledger and restock reminders remain device-local. We do not sell your personal data. You can delete your account and data at any time in-app or via our online deletion request page.
