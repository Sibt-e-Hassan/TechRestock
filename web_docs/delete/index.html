Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: July 16, 2026

This page explains how you can request the deletion of your Bazaar Connect account and associated data, what data is deleted, and what (if anything) is retained. Bazaar Connect is a marketplace discovery and inquiry app; the developer can be reached at raxyshan@gmail.com.

Option 1 — Delete from within the app (instant)

  • Open the Bazaar Connect app and sign in.
  • Go to the Account tab.
  • Tap Delete account.
  • Confirm with your password. Your account and data are removed immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email raxyshan@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject “Bazaar Connect account deletion request.” We will verify your identity and process the request within 30 days.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, the following are permanently removed:

  • Your sign-in account (email and authentication credentials).
  • Your profile record (full name, email, display name, and preferences).
  • Your saved cart items.
  • Your inquiry / order records linked to your user ID.

What data is retained

Bazaar Connect does not sell your data or use it for advertising. After account deletion, we do not retain personal data tied to your account, except where a short retention period is required by law or to prevent fraud and abuse. Any such records are kept no longer than 90 days and are then permanently erased. Anonymised, aggregated statistics that cannot identify you may be retained.

Note: The public catalog of markets, shops, and products is not personal data and is unaffected by account deletion. Account deletion cannot be undone.

Contact

For any questions about account or data deletion, contact us at raxyshan@gmail.com.
